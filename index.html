About

Turning complex evidence into stronger care.

My work brings together pharmaceutical microbiology, bioinformatics and implementation research to improve treatment effectiveness in African health systems. I use molecular and genomic evidence alongside routine clinical, surveillance and supply-chain data—building analyses that can travel from laboratory and database to programme and policy.

My scientific portfolio spans HIV drug resistance, antimicrobial consumption and resistance, sickle cell genomics, molecular diagnostics, microbiome research, pharmacoepidemiology and health informatics. Across these areas, capacity building is central: sustainable data platforms, local analytical expertise and mentorship are part of the research itself.
