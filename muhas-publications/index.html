Methods and cautions

Broad at inception; curated thereafter.

The extraction uses OpenAlex institution lineage I154840374 and ROR 027pr6c67. Records are retained broadly at inception. School and department fields remain blank until MUHAS validates a time-aware author affiliation crosswalk.

OpenAlex records can be incomplete, delayed or incorrectly affiliated. Counts are therefore provisional research-intelligence evidence, not an audited institutional return, until curation is completed.
